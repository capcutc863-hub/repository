# **App Name**: Emerald Sales

## Core Features:

- User Management: Role-based access control (Admin, Supervisor, Sales) with user activation/deactivation and online status monitoring.
- Sales Rep Configuration: Configure maximum discount limits and variable commission rates for each sales representative.
- Invoice Generation: Smart interface for adding products, specifying quantities (in mg), and prices. Also support discount and shipping costs
- Invoice Output: Printable invoice and PNG conversion with invoice status tracking (Pending, Delivered, Returned).
- Inventory Management: Categorize oils by quality (Grade A, B, C) and track inventory levels with stock progress bars and low stock alerts. Automatically deduct quantities upon invoice generation.
- AI Marketing: Gemini AI tool analyzes customer purchase history to generate re-marketing messages and product suggestions to encourage repeat orders.
- Team Chat: Real-time chat system for coordination between sales reps and management, to streamline order processing and logistics.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Emerald green (#50C878) to reflect the theme of aromatic oils and luxury.
- Background color: Desaturated light green (#E8F8EF) for a calm and sophisticated feel.
- Accent color: Gold (#FFD700) for highlights, buttons, and important information.
- Headline and body font: 'Cairo' (sans-serif) for a modern yet elegant Arabic script aesthetic. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Lucide Icons will be used, in the 'feather' style.
- Modern layout with large, curved corners (3rem), soft shadows, and a focus on a responsive design.
- Subtle animations for opening menus and pop-up windows to enhance user experience.
